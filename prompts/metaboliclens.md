# MetabolicLens — project thumbnail prompt

Realistic, professional product mockup for a portfolio project card. 16:9 crop (480x270 or similar), clean modern health/wellness mobile-app aesthetic, NOT a flat abstract vector icon.

**What it is:** MetabolicLens, a metabolic-health tracking mobile app (React Native). AI-powered food-photo analysis that computes an "Insulin Load Score" (0-100) from a photo of a meal, using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. Features: camera capture, gallery picker, meal history, fasting tracker.

**Visual direction:**
- Realistic smartphone mockup (modern iPhone-style device, held at a slight angle or standing upright) displaying a clean health-app UI.
- Screen shows: a photo of a real, appetizing meal (e.g. a plate of grilled chicken, rice, and vegetables) with a circular "Insulin Load Score" gauge/ring overlaid (e.g. showing "42/100" in a friendly color-coded ring: green=low, amber=medium, red=high), plus a small "fasting timer" widget below (e.g. "14:32 elapsed") and a meal-history strip of thumbnail photos at the bottom.
- Primary brand accent: bottle green (#006a4e) for the score ring and buttons, on a clean white/cream (#fffaf0) app background, soft rounded 16-24px corners matching a "clay" design language.
- Soft, natural lighting on the phone mockup, subtle shadow, professional health-tech marketing-shot style (think Noom/MyFitnessPal/Oura-style app screenshots).
- No invented medical claims text, keep UI labels generic/legible only if needed.

**Avoid:** cartoonish icons, flat vector food illustrations, clutter, low-res look, gimmicky stock photos of tape measures or scales.
